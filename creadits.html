Preservation Communities & Archivists

This archive would not exist without the dedication of the following communities and individuals who spent years finding, dumping, and preserving these files:

  • Macintosh Repository — Special thanks to that-ben, JDW, Amid, and InkBlot for the All Macintosh ROMs collection.
  • MacGUI Forums — For the discovery, restoration, and dumping of the 1983 Twiggy Macintosh prototype and emulator patches.
  • WinWorldPC — For providing clean, verified system software releases (System 1.0 through 7).
  • Apple Lisa Preservation Project — For ongoing research into early Apple hardware.

Special Thanks

  • To the original engineers at Apple who created these machines and software.
  • To everyone in the global retro-computing community who continues to test, document, and keep this history alive.
