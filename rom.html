Pre-release Prototypes (1983)
Note on the Twiggy ROM dump:
These are the raw ROM dump files from the Macintosh Twiggy prototype owned by Adam Goolevitch, and provided to David Finnigan at Mac GUI Vault for disassembly and analysis in February 2014.

"Except for changes in the disk driver code, the biggest fundamental difference between Twiggy ROM and Macintosh 128K ROM is a difference in the format of resources. This difference prevents utilities like ResEdit from properly being able to analyze and display the resources in the Twiggy ROM and System Software."
— D. Finnigan (13 Nov 2015)
